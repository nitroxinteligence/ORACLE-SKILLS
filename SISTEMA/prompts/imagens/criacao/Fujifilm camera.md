==================================================
TARGET COLOR-GRADE
==================================================

Create a bright, vivid, clean photographic look with:

- stronger color separation
- moderately stronger contrast
- brighter midtones
- slightly opened shadows
- protected highlights
- clean whites
- vivid but controlled colors
- warm, luminous skin
- cyan-leaning blues
- vivid vermilion / orange-red reds
- a subtle warm and slightly green-biased white balance

The image should feel fresh, sunny, transparent and colorful.

It must NOT look faded, matte, washed out, hazy, vintage, brown, heavily cinematic, HDR-like, or artificially overprocessed.

==================================================
1. TONAL RESPONSE
==================================================

Increase the perceived exposure moderately.

Brighten the midtones noticeably while retaining highlight detail.

Lift shadow detail moderately, especially on faces, clothing and environmental details, but preserve real dark areas and a solid black anchor.

Do NOT flatten the photograph.

Maintain a clear difference between:
- highlights
- midtones
- shadows
- blacks

Highlights should remain bright and clean without clipping.

White clothing, clouds and bright surfaces should become slightly brighter and cleaner.

Dark hair, dark clothing and deep shadows should remain convincingly dark instead of turning gray.

The overall tonal result should have more punch and transparency than the original while remaining natural.

Use moderately strong global contrast and restrained local contrast.

Avoid:
- crushed blacks
- blown highlights
- gray lifted blacks
- fake HDR
- halos
- excessive clarity
- oversharpening

==================================================
2. WHITE BALANCE
==================================================

Shift the overall image moderately warmer.

At the same time, reduce magenta noticeably and bias the tint slightly toward green.

This warm-plus-green balance is a critical part of the target look.

The result should NOT visibly look green.

Instead, this color balance should subtly produce:

- warmer skin
- cleaner yellow-peach highlights
- reds that lean more orange / vermilion
- blues that lean more cyan
- whites that feel slightly creamy rather than cold blue

Neutral whites should remain close to neutral with only a subtle warm ivory character.

Do NOT create a strong yellow cast.

Do NOT create a visible green cast on skin.

==================================================
3. BLUE / CYAN COLOR RESPONSE
==================================================

Blue is one of the most important characteristics of this grade.

Transform deep or slightly violet blues toward a cleaner cyan-blue / turquoise-blue direction.

For skies, water, blue walls, blue clothing and similar blue regions:

- shift blue hue moderately toward cyan
- increase cyan presence
- reduce purple / violet contamination
- retain rich blue color
- keep sufficient saturation
- slightly increase luminance where appropriate

A deep royal-blue sky should become a brighter, cleaner cyan-blue sky.

The blue should feel similar to:
clear summer sky,
Fuji-like cyan blue,
fresh turquoise-leaning blue.

Do NOT make blue:
- navy
- purple
- neon turquoise
- electric cyan
- gray
- heavily desaturated

Keep the transformation photographic and believable.

==================================================
4. RED / ORANGE COLOR RESPONSE
==================================================

Red is the second major signature color.

Transform cool crimson or magenta-leaning red toward a brighter vermilion / orange-red.

For red objects, clothing, decorations, flowers and similar regions:

- increase red-orange luminosity
- increase richness and saturation
- reduce magenta contamination
- shift cool red slightly toward warm red / vermilion
- retain texture and highlight information

The final red should feel energetic, warm and vivid.

Think:

deep red
→ vivid vermilion / warm orange-red

NOT:

deep red
→ pink

NOT:

deep red
→ fluorescent orange

Highly saturated red areas may become noticeably more vivid, but they must retain texture and must not clip into a flat block of color.

==================================================
5. SKIN TONE
==================================================

Protect human skin very carefully.

Skin should become:

- slightly brighter
- warmer
- clearer
- more luminous
- subtly golden
- peach-yellow rather than pink-magenta

Reduce excessive pink / magenta in skin.

Keep natural red variation in cheeks and lips.

Do NOT make skin:
- orange
- yellow
- green
- waxy
- overly smooth
- artificially white
- heavily retouched

Preserve the person's real identity and real skin texture.

Skin should look like the same person photographed under cleaner, warmer daylight.

==================================================
6. WHITE AND NEUTRAL COLORS
==================================================

Neutral colors should become cleaner and slightly warmer.

Cold blue-gray whites may move gently toward warm ivory / creamy white.

White clothing and clouds should remain visually white.

Gray areas should retain neutrality.

Avoid contaminating neutral areas with obvious:
- green
- cyan
- orange
- yellow

The warmth must remain subtle.

==================================================
7. SATURATION
==================================================

Apply only a small global saturation increase.

Apply a stronger vibrance-style enhancement.

Prioritize improving colors that benefit from greater separation instead of increasing every color equally.

The final image should be visibly more colorful than the original.

Particularly strengthen:
- red / orange
- cyan / blue
- healthy skin warmth

Maintain believable photographic color.

Do NOT create:
- neon colors
- fluorescent colors
- extreme social-media saturation
- posterized colors
- clipped color channels

==================================================
8. FINISH
==================================================

Add only a very subtle natural vignette around the extreme outer edges.

The vignette should almost disappear when viewed normally.

Do NOT add:
- film grain
- dust
- scratches
- light leaks
- bloom
- glow
- diffusion
- faded blacks
- vintage texture
- analog frame
- fake film borders

Keep the photograph clean and modern.
