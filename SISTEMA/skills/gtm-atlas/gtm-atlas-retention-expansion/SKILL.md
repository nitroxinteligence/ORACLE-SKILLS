# GTM Atlas Retention and Expansion

Treat retention as a company-wide operating system and expansion as the result of repeatedly proving value in the customer's terms.

## Workflow

1. Diagnose retention across product, sales, implementation, support, and executive ownership. Do not isolate churn inside the CS team.
2. Identify the second commitment after the initial deal: renewed usage, another team, another workflow, or another purchase. Use it as evidence that value travels beyond the first sale.
3. Define the customer's business objectives and co-build a transformation plan with specific workflows, owners, controls, and impact measures.
4. Deliver an early working outcome through a workshop or implementation session. Leave the customer able to operate and extend the workflow.
5. Segment service into dedicated, scaled, and digital coverage. Allocate human help through usage, adoption, risk, complexity, and expansion signals.
6. Build enablement that teaches customers to succeed without permanent dependence on a forward-deployed expert.
7. Measure three layers: top-line outcomes such as ARR and NRR; leading indicators such as meaningful usage and active teams; and value-driving activities such as plans, workshops, and reviews.
8. Translate value into the customer's own terms, such as time saved, faster shipping, risk reduced, revenue gained, or onboarding improved.
9. Feed recurring signals into product and marketing, and make new value visible to customers.

## Guardrails

- Speed and responsiveness are renewal signals; define ownership and response standards.
- Do not confuse activity or logins with business value.
- Avoid a high-touch model that cannot scale or leaves the customer dependent on one person.
- Plan for the relationship and product footprint two years ahead while prioritizing the current workflow.

## Output

Return: retention diagnosis, second-commitment signal, transformation plan, coverage model, value metrics, health signals, renewal and expansion plays, enablement assets, owners, and review cadence.
