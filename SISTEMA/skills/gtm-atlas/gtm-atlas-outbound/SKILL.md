# GTM Atlas Outbound

Build outbound in the order: reliable data, ICP, intent, narrative, timing, channel, and measurement. Better copy cannot rescue the wrong account or stale signal.

## Workflow

1. Define the customer pain, account fit, relevant personas, buying roles, and expected revenue.
2. Audit the data needed to identify and contact those accounts. Mark freshness, provenance, confidence, and ownership.
3. Define intent and timing signals that change what to say now: product behavior, hiring, funding, executive changes, regulation, technology, or competitive shifts.
4. Tier accounts by fit, intent, and value. Use value to scale research depth and human attention.
5. Centralize repeatable enrichment and pre-call research. Give reps concise, verified context and remove work between conversations.
6. Match narrative altitude to the recipient. Users may care about product experience; budget owners need business impact, risk, and urgency.
7. Coordinate email, phone, and professional social channels when the audience uses them.
8. Run each campaign as an explicit hypothesis with a segment, signal, narrative, channel mix, sample size, success metric, and stop condition.

## Guardrails

- Do not use enthusiasm, opens, or generic engagement as proof of buying intent.
- Require a painful problem and a credible reason to act within the relevant horizon.
- Do not force activity quotas that exhaust poor-fit accounts.
- Assign one owner for production quality, feedback intake, and system-wide deployment.
- Use AI to remove research and administrative waste; preserve human judgment for consequential outreach and discovery.

## Output

Return: target segment, data requirements, scoring model, signal library, research brief template, narrative by role, sequence, experiment design, and operating owner.
