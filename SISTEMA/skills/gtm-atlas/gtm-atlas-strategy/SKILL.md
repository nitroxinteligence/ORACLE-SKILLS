# GTM Atlas Strategy

Build a GTM system from the company's beliefs about customers and buying behavior. Treat tools, org charts, and named methodologies as implementation choices rather than the strategy itself.

## Workflow

1. Establish the business objective, stage, runway, current motion, product economics, and available evidence.
2. State the customer problem, who feels it most, what outcome they seek, and why the problem matters now.
3. List the critical GTM assumptions. Separate observed facts from hypotheses.
4. Choose a small set of bets across capture, qualification, outbound, activation, pricing, conversion, retention, and expansion. Include only stages relevant to the current constraint.
5. For each bet, define the expected behavior change, leading indicator, business outcome, cost, owner, and kill or revise condition.
6. Sequence the bets so the team learns before it scales. Prefer fast evidence over elaborate infrastructure.
7. Produce a short operating plan and a decision log that can be updated as evidence arrives.

## Decision rules

- Preserve fundamentals: customer value and revenue remain the objective even when AI changes execution speed.
- Do not copy another company's motion without explaining why its assumptions transfer.
- Use the company's distinct data, access, product experience, and customer insight as sources of advantage.
- Keep a clear point of view, but mark uncertainty and design tests that can disprove it.
- Avoid a universal maturity model. Start at the bottleneck the user actually has.

## Output

Return: strategic thesis, customer and problem, evidence, assumptions, prioritized bets, experiment cards, metrics, owners, dependencies, and next review date.
