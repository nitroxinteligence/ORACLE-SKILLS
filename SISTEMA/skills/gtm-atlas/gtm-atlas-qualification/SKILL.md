# GTM Atlas Qualification

Qualify whole accounts through evidence, timing, and revenue potential. Build reusable context so research and decisions compound.

## Workflow

1. Start from actual customers, call notes, wins, losses, product telemetry, and buying events. Label missing evidence.
2. Distinguish the early customer profile from the longer-term ideal customer profile. Favor early adopters with urgent pain, manageable procurement, and willingness to co-design.
3. Score four layers: company and persona fit; behaviors linked to conversion or value; timing events that create a why-now window; and revenue potential that determines effort.
4. Reverse-engineer weights from the customers the business wants more of. Exclude exceptional one-off accounts from the baseline.
5. Track milestones at the account level: awareness, meaningful engagement, evaluation, buying process, adoption, and expansion. Do not treat marketing-to-sales movement as a linear handoff.
6. Create or update a compact GTM context containing ICP summary, positioning, priorities, signal library, battlecards, messaging matrix, workflows, and dated outputs.
7. Schedule a review when enough new evidence accumulates; quarterly is a useful default for an active early-stage motion.

## Decision rules

- Content engagement counts only when it predicts the desired outcome.
- Account activity must aggregate multiple people and roles without losing individual context.
- Keep content creation and distribution connected; evaluate campaigns as fuel plus engine.
- Optimize the website for informed visitors and conversion while keeping machine-readable facts accessible.

## Output

Return: ECP, ICP, exclusions, scoring rubric, signal definitions, account milestones, routing by revenue potential, evidence gaps, and context-maintenance plan.
